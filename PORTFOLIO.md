# Patrícia Cordeiro – Digital heritage portfolio

Selected projects in the digital documentation and mediation of cultural heritage, with a focus on
intangible heritage, 3D and community participation. For each project I state my role: what I
coordinated and what I did myself.

**Web version:** https://cordpatricia.github.io/vimioso-3d/ ·
**Hands-on 3D work:** [Cântara MECC.0193](README.md)

## Projects (chronological order)

### Carnival of Podence – UNESCO nomination and digital archive (2017–2019)
- **My role:** coordinator of the nomination; designer of the digital components (independent consultant).
- Coordination of the nomination inscribed on the UNESCO Representative List of the Intangible Cultural
  Heritage of Humanity in 2019, and design of an associated heritage archive, a digital game and an audio
  guide ("Chocalhar Podence"; digital components produced by ESACT-IPB).
- Link: [UNESCO listing](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/winter-festivities-carnival-of-podence-01463)

### National Inventory of Intangible Cultural Heritage, Portugal (2017–2024)
- **My role:** preparation of the applications.
- Applications approved and published in the Diário da República: Carnival of Podence; Festa de Santo
  Estêvão, Torre de Dona Chama; Carnival of Lazarim.

### Masked Rituals of the Lazarim Carnival (2021–2024)
- **My role:** coordinator (MORE CoLAB); Municipality of Lamego, with Blaster Image Works.
- Digital inventory of intangible heritage, 3D capture of masks, and audio and video recordings for a
  multimedia catalogue of the Iberian Mask Interpretation Centre (CIMI).

### EscarpArte – rock art of the Passos/Santa Comba mountains (2021–2027)
- **My role:** project manager (MORE CoLAB, lead organisation); author of the app's content and narrative.
- 3D and multispectral survey of painted rock shelters, 3D capture of objects and a pilot AR mobile app
  ("la Caixa" Foundation and FCT). The 3D survey was carried out by specialist partners; I wrote the app's
  content and narrative, combining scientific texts with community testimonies and local history.
- Links: [project website](https://escarparte.pt/) · [MORE CoLAB](https://morecolab.pt/en/project-escaparte/) ·
  [3D models on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/MORECOLAB)

### Dancing into the Digital Age – Pauliteiros folk dances (2023)
- **My role:** researcher, co-author and community mediator (MORE CoLAB and ESACT-IPB).
- Experimental model for the digitisation of traditional folk dances. I mediated the group's
  participation: selection of the choreography, annotation and documentation of the costumes.
- Links: [paper, CISTI 2023](https://doi.org/10.23919/CISTI58278.2023.10211933) ·
  [open repository](https://bibliotecadigital.ipb.pt/entities/publication/86d08783-84aa-4f3c-910b-900b3086d096)

### Cultural Tourism Workshops – Macedo de Cavaleiros (2025)
- **My role:** project coordinator; follow-up of the Horizon 2020 project TExTOUR, in which I represented
  the Macedo de Cavaleiros territory.
- 9 co-design sessions with 78 residents in 8 villages, a local heritage database, a co-created visual
  identity and a pilot test with 15 participants.

### Casa da Cultura de Vimioso – Between Memories and Pixels (2025–2027)
- **My role:** coordinator (MORE CoLAB); Municipality of Vimioso, NORTE 2030.
- Digitisation of an ethnographic collection, its integration into Europeana, and research and digital
  documentation of the intangible heritage associated with the objects.
- My own hands-on 3D documentation of one object: [Cântara MECC.0193](README.md)
