Cântara (water vessel), MECC.0193 – 3D documentation

Casa da Cultura de Vimioso · October 2026 · in progress

My role: capture, processing, metadata and publication, done by me

Photogrammetric capture of a clay vessel (iPad, three captures), scale from a ruler in the scene, orientation and checks in Blender, publication on Sketchfab, paradata and an EDM record for Europeana, linked to community memory recorded in interviews.

EscarpArte – rock art of the Passos/Santa Comba mountains

“la Caixa” Foundation and FCT · 2021–2027

My role: project manager; author of the app's content and narrative

3D and multispectral survey of painted rock shelters, 3D capture of objects and a pilot AR mobile app. I wrote the content and narrative, combining scientific texts with community testimonies and local history.

My role: researcher, co-author and community mediator

Experimental model for the digitisation of traditional folk dances. I mediated the group's participation: selection of the choreography, annotation and documentation of the costumes.
